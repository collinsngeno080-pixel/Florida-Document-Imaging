About

A Lit & More solution

Florida Document Imaging is the document scanning service of Lit & More, Inc., a Florida litigation support company since 2001.

Where it started

Litigation work leaves no room for a missed page. Years ago a client asked us to bring that same standard to their business records. That project became our back-file scanning service.

Most of our team has been with us for more than ten years.

Who you work with

A project manager who oversees the job from pickup to delivery, and a production team in Fort Myers that does the scanning. You talk to the same people throughout.
