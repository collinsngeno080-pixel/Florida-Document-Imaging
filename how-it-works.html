How it works

From box to PDF

Four steps, handled by our own staff from the day we collect your boxes to the day your files are delivered.

  1. Pickup

    Our staff collect the boxes. No couriers. Every box gets a tracking label.

  2. Inventory

    We log each box file by file, so you know what came in and where it went.

  3. Scan

    Every page, every sheet. Post-its and notes inside folders included. Large plans scanned separately.

  4. Deliver

    Searchable PDFs named from your folder labels. Originals come back to you or are shredded with a certificate.

The details

Pickup
Collected by our trained staff, never a courier. Each box gets a tracking label.
Inventory
Logged file by file with break sheets, so every page can be traced back to its box.
Scanning
300 DPI black and white as standard. Color when the page needs it. Oversize plans kept separate and named to match their file.
Things we can’t scan
Media and objects get a numbered placeholder in the file, and the same number on the item. They come back separately.
File names
Taken from your folder labels under one short standard. Folders without labels are numbered.
Delivery
Searchable PDFs through a secure online share, or on a drive.
Originals
Returned in the original box, or shredded with a certificate of destruction.
Timing
Most projects finish in about 30 days. Your quote includes the date.
