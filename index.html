How it works

From box to PDF

  1. Pickup

    Our staff collect the boxes. No couriers. Every box gets a tracking label.

  2. Inventory

    We log each box file by file, so you know what came in and where it went.

  3. Scan

    Every page, every sheet. Post-its and notes inside folders included. Large plans scanned separately.

  4. Deliver

    Searchable PDFs named from your folder labels. Originals come back to you or are shredded with a certificate.
